About

I use experimental design, big data analytics, machine learning, and advanced statistical modeling to examine consumer behavior in hospitality and tourism.

My methodological approach integrates theory-driven inquiry with large-scale data analysis and data-intensive evidence to examine consumer decision making across hospitality environments, lodging experiences, digital platforms, and AI-mediated service contexts.

Through this approach, my research aims to advance hospitality theory, offer practical guidance for industry practice, and contribute to societal discussions about how digital platforms and AI systems shape consumer choice, service experiences, and value creation in hospitality and tourism.

Research Interests

My research interests include hospitality analytics, AI-mediated decision making, digital platforms, and lodging experiences.

Hospitality Analytics

Applies big data analytics, machine learning, and advanced statistical modeling to examine consumer behavior and industry trends in hospitality and tourism.

AI-Mediated Decision Making

Investigates how AI-generated information, algorithmic recommendations, transparency, and trust influence consumer judgment and behavior.

Digital Platforms

Studies how online platforms, review systems, search interfaces, and platform-mediated information environments shape consumer decisions.

Lodging Experiences

Explores how hotel environments, service design, and experiential cues shape guest perception, memory, satisfaction, and behavioral outcomes.
