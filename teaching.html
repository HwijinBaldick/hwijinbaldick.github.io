Teaching Philosophy

My teaching emphasizes conceptual clarity, analytical reasoning, and student engagement in hospitality and tourism management. I apply a flipped classroom approach to encourage active learning and to help students connect theory, quantitative thinking, and managerial decision making in service-industry contexts.

Across accounting, operations analysis, and lodging management courses, I aim to help students develop practical problem-solving skills through applied examples, Excel-based exercises, and real-world service industry scenarios.
